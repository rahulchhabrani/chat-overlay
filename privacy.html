Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Multistream Chat Overlay is a free, open-source Chrome extension. This page explains what data the extension does and does not collect.

What we collect

The extension stores the following data locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local:

• Twitch channel name (entered by you in the popup)
• Kick channel name (entered by you in the popup)
• Your chosen target website (e.g. chess.com)
• Recent chat messages (for history across page refreshes)

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, database, or third party.

Third-party connections

When you enter a channel name and connect, the extension opens a direct WebSocket connection from your browser to the platform's own servers:

• Twitch: wss://irc-ws.chat.twitch.tv
• Kick: wss://ws-us2.pusher.com
• YouTube: reads chat from an open YouTube tab in your browser

These connections are governed by each platform's own privacy policy. The extension does not intercept, log, or forward any data from these connections.

What we do not collect

• We do not collect personal information
• We do not use analytics or tracking
• We do not use cookies
• We do not share any data with third parties
• We do not require you to create an account or log in

Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

• storage — to save your channel names and settings locally
• tabs — to detect open YouTube stream tabs for chat relay
• scripting / content scripts — to inject the chat overlay on your chosen website
• host permissions — to run on the sites you configure

Open source

The full source code is available on GitHub. You can inspect exactly what the extension does.

Contact

If you have questions, open an issue on GitHub.
