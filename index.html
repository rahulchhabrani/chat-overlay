Install in 4 steps

1
Download

Click the Download button above and unzip the file anywhere on your computer.

2
Enable Developer Mode

Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and toggle Developer mode on (top-right corner).

3
Load the extension

Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped chat-overlay-main folder.

4
Connect your channels

Click the Chat Overlay icon in your toolbar, enter your Twitch / Kick / YouTube channel, and hit Save & Connect.

How it works

Twitch
Native IRC

Connects directly to Twitch’s IRC over WebSocket. No login or OAuth token required.

Kick
Pusher WebSocket

Connects to Kick’s Pusher API in real time. Instant message delivery, no polling.

YouTube
DOM Relay

Reads chat from an open YouTube stream tab and relays it to the overlay automatically.

Why it works everywhere
No iframes

Most overlays use iframes which get blocked by strict CSPs. Direct WebSockets bypass that — works on chess.com, gaming sites, anywhere.
